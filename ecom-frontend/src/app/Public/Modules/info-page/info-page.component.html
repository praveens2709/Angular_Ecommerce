Shipping charges

Shipping is free on every order, with no minimum order value.

Where we deliver

We deliver across India. Enter your pincode on any product page to check delivery to your area. A few remote pincodes may not be serviceable yet; you'll see this before you order.

Dispatch and delivery times

Orders are packed and handed to our courier partners within 1–2 business days. Estimated delivery after dispatch:

DestinationEstimated delivery
Same city as our warehouseAbout 2 business days
Same stateAbout 3 business days
Rest of IndiaAbout 5 business days
North-East, Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh and islandsUp to 7 business days

These are estimates. Sundays, public holidays, weather and courier delays can add time.

Cash on delivery

Cash on delivery (cash or UPI to the delivery partner) is available on all serviceable pincodes.

Tracking your order

Follow every step (ordered, shipped, delivered) under Account › Orders. We also email you when your order ships and when it's delivered.

Failed delivery

If the courier can't reach you after repeated attempts, the parcel returns to us and the order is cancelled. Any prepaid amount is refunded as described in our Returns & Refunds policy.

Cancelling an order

You can cancel any order before it ships from the order page in Account › Orders. Once an order has shipped it can't be cancelled, but you can return it after delivery.

Returns and exchanges

  • Request a return or a size exchange within {{ store.returnWindowDays }} days of delivery, from the order page.
  • Items must be unused and unwashed, with original tags and packaging.
  • Innerwear may not be returnable for hygiene reasons unless it arrived damaged or incorrect.
  • Exchanges are for a different size of the same product, subject to stock. The replacement ships free.

How it works

  1. Open the order and choose Return or Exchange, then tell us why.
  2. We review the request (usually within 1–2 business days) and arrange a pickup or share return instructions.
  3. Once the item reaches us and passes a quality check, we approve the refund or ship the exchange.

Refunds

  • Prepaid orders are refunded to the original payment method within 5–7 business days of approval.
  • Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer or UPI; we'll ask for your details.
  • Coupon discounts are not refunded as cash.

Damaged or wrong item?

Contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos and your order ID, and we'll make it right at no cost to you.

This policy explains what personal data {{ store.legalName || store.name }} ("we") collects when you use this website, why, and the choices you have. It is written with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 in mind.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email, mobile number, gender and (optionally) date of birth.
  • Delivery addresses you save.
  • Orders: items, amounts, payment method, delivery status, returns.
  • Saved cards: only the cardholder name, card type, expiry and the last 4 digits. We never store full card numbers or CVV.
  • Reviews, wishlist and messages you send us.
  • Technical data such as IP address and browser type, used for security and to prevent abuse.

Why we use it

  • To process, deliver and support your orders, returns and refunds.
  • To send order confirmations, delivery updates and password-reset emails.
  • To keep your account secure and prevent fraud.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations (for example GST invoices).

We do not sell your personal data.

Who we share it with

Only with service providers who help us run the store, and only what they need:

  • Courier partners (name, address, phone) to deliver your order.
  • Payment providers to process payments.
  • Email, hosting, database and image-storage providers that operate our systems.
  • Authorities, when required by law.

Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and to remember your delivery pincode. We don't use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept while your account is active. Order and invoice records are kept as long as tax laws require, even after an account is deleted.

Your rights

  • View and correct your details under Account › Profile and Addresses.
  • Delete your account under Account › Delete Account.
  • Ask us about, or object to, how we use your data by contacting us.

Security

Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, sessions expire automatically, and access to customer data is restricted to authorised staff.
